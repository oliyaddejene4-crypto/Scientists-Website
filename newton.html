Isaac Newton

  • The father of classical physics and the laws of motion.

    • Biography

  • Sir Isaac Newton (1643–1727) was an English mathematician, physicist, astronomer, and author widely recognized as one of the most influential scientists in history.

  • His work laid the foundation of classical mechanics, and his discoveries helped explain the motion of planets, objects on Earth, and the nature of gravity.

    • Newton’s Laws of Motion

    1. First Law (Inertia): An object remains at rest or in motion unless acted upon by a force.
    2. Second Law: F = ma (Force equals mass times acceleration).
    3. Third Law: For every action, there is an equal and opposite reaction.

    Law of Universal Gravitation

  • Newton discovered that every object in the universe attracts every other object with a force:

    • F=G(m₁m₂/r²)

  • This explained why planets orbit the Sun and why objects fall to the ground.

    Fun Facts

    1. He discovered gravity after observing a falling apple (legend).
    2. He invented calculus independently.
    3. He was deeply interested in alchemy.
    4. He worked at the Royal Mint in England.
    5. He never married.
