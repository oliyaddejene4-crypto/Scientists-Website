Nikola Tesla

  • The genius inventor who powered the modern world with electricity.

    • Biography

  • Nikola Tesla (1856–1943) was a Serbian-American inventor, electrical engineer, mechanical engineer, and futurist best known for his contributions to the development of alternating current (AC) electrical systems.

  • Born in Smiljan (modern-day Croatia), Tesla later moved to the United States where he worked with Thomas Edison before becoming one of history’s most independent and visionary inventors.

    • Major Inventions & Contributions

    1. Alternating Current (AC) System
    2. Tesla Coil
    3. Induction Motor
    4. Wireless Communication Concepts
    5. Remote Control Technology
    6. Radio Technology (early development)

    Timeline of Tesla’s Life

    Year Event
    1856 Born in Smiljan, Austrian Empire
    1884 Moved to the United States
    1887 Developed AC induction motor
    1893 Demonstrated wireless lighting
    1901 Started building Wardenclyffe Tower
    1943 Died in New York City

    Fun Facts About Tesla

    1. He claimed to have a photographic memory.
    2. He could visualize inventions completely in his mind.
    3. He loved pigeons and cared for injured birds.
    4. He never married and dedicated his life to science.
    5. He worked in rivalry with Thomas Edison.
