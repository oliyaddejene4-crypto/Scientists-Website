Albert Einstein

  • The man who changed physics forever with the theory of relativity.

    • Biography

  • Albert Einstein (1879–1955) was a German-born theoretical physicist, widely considered one of the greatest scientists of all time. He is best known for developing the theory of relativity, which revolutionized modern physics and changed how we understand space, time, and gravity.

  • He worked in Switzerland, Germany, and later the United States. In 1921, he received the Nobel Prize in Physics for his explanation of the photoelectric effect.

    • Major Scientific Contributions

    • The Theory of Relativity (Special & General Relativity)
    • Mass–Energy Equivalence (E = mc²)
    • Photoelectric Effect
    • Brownian Motion Explanation
    • Gravitational Theory of Space-Time
